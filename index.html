Research Experience

Research Intern | Pennsylvania State University

Supervised by Prof. Binghai Yan, Department of Physics (July 2025 - Present)

Project Topic: Examining Charge Density Wave (CDW) and possible superconductivity in novel Monolayer material

  • Identified a CDW-perturbed structure by applying symmetry analysis and targeted structural perturbations.
  • Generated simulated Scanning Tunneling Microscopy (STM) images, observing a characteristic contrast inversion in the periodic charge distribution that successfully matched experimental results.
  • Surveying literature and consulting prior collaborators to identify tractable ways to explore exciton properties in large supercell.

Research Intern | University of Cambridge

Supervised by Dr. Bo Peng, Theory of Condensed Matter Group (UoC) (December 2024 - Present)

Project-1: Unconventional superconductivity in layered fullerene structures (Working with Dr. Ruoshi Jiang)

  • Investigated evidence of strong electron correlation within the target structure.
  • Built and validated a tight-binding model treating the C60 buckyball as a carbon super-atom, enabling further calculations and analysis of the system.

Project-2: Design of novel 2D ferroelectric materials using buckyballs

  • Proposed possible chemical compositions and crystal structures based on previous theoretical assumptions.
  • Identified a few compatible FE candidates with reasonable polarization by performing first-principles calculations using VASP, Wannier90, and Quantum Espresso.
  • Determined that charge transfer between central ions and the buckyball cage was the mechanism responsible for reduced polarization in other candidate structures.

Summer Research | University of Cambridge

Supervised by Dr. Bo Peng, Theory of Condensed Matter Group (UoC) (June 2024 - October 2024)

Project-1: Tuning electronic and optical properties of 2D polymeric C60 by stacking two layers.

  • Contributed to DFT calculations for electronic band structure and photo-absorption properties.
  • Found stronger exciton absorption in the bilayer than that in the monolayer over the entire visible light range, proposing the bilayer material as a more promising candidate for photovoltaic applications.

Summer Research (Project-2) | University of Cambridge

Co-advised by Prof. Daniel Bennett (NTU, Harvard) and working with Dr. Cameron A.M. Scott & Prof. Nicholas Bristowe (Durham) (June 2024 - Present)

Project: Structural stability and Symmetry analysis of novel bismuth oxide ferroelectric

  • Determined the stable bulk structure of Bismuth Oxide (Bi6O9) by combining a group-theory-based vibrational analysis with first-principles calculations.
  • Identified the coupling of order parameters within the Landau Free Energy, suggesting that the material's properties are tunable via variation of applied strain or electric fields.
  • Presented initial findings and theoretical analysis in a poster presentation at the Lennard-Jones Centre, University of Cambridge.

Summer Research | Nanjing University

(July 2023 - September 2023)

Project Topic: Growth, critical property analysis, phase transition modulation, and ferroelectric domain studies of Perovskite single crystal

  • Designed and built customized sample holders.
  • Conducted ferroelectric hysteresis tests across different temperatures to determine the Curie temperature and cryogenic behaviors of the perovskite structure, analyzing the results against theoretical predictions.
